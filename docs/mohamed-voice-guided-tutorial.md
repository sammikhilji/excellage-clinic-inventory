# Clinic Inventory — Mohamed Voice-Guided Tutorial (Filming Script)

**App URL:** https://excellage-clinic-inventory.vercel.app  
**On-screen person:** Mohamed (Admin) — username `mohamed`  
**Password:** enter Mohamed’s password from the staff handout (do **not** speak or show a real password on camera)  
**Format:** Portrait phone · warm clinic training voice · one step at a time  
**Audience:** Nurses / stock users watching Mohamed demonstrate

Address staff as **“you”** while Mohamed shows the screen.

---

## Scene 1 — Open app / first-time login

**Scene title:** First-time login (username, not email)

**On screen:** Open Safari/Chrome → https://excellage-clinic-inventory.vercel.app → Clinic login form (Username + Password)

**Voiceover:**  
“Welcome to Clinic Inventory. Open the clinic app link on your phone. You’ll see Clinic login. Type your **staff username** — not an email. For this walkthrough, Mohamed signs in with username **mohamed**. Enter Mohamed’s password from the staff handout. Tap **Sign in**.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on the login screen after username is filled · 1 second after Sign in · wait for Home to appear

---

## Scene 2 — Change password

**Scene title:** Change my password

**On screen:** Top bar → tap **Password** → `/password` → “Change my password” form (Current password, New password, Confirm)

**Voiceover:**  
“After you sign in, look at the top bar and tap **Password**. Here you can change your own password. Type your current password, choose a new one, and confirm it. For this filming we only show the screen — do **not** tap Update password unless you really want to change it. Tap **Home** or the back link when you’re done.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on the Password form · optional 1 second hover over Update password without tapping

**Note for Mohamed:** Path is **Password** in the header → `/password`. Admins also see “reset a staff password” lower on the same page — skip that for the staff version of this video, or mention briefly: “Admins can reset staff passwords on this same page.”

---

## Scene 3 — Home overview

**Scene title:** Home — products, quantity, expiry, activity

**On screen:** Home `/` — Products count · Total qty · Stock by location · Expiry alerts · Recent activity (with dates)

**Voiceover:**  
“This is Home — your daily overview. At the top you’ll see how many products you have and the total quantity across the clinic. Below that is stock by location — Main Store and each doctor room. Scroll to **Expiry alerts** for items that need attention. Further down, **Recent activity** shows adds, moves, and use — with the **date** and time, so you can see what happened today.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on totals · slow scroll through locations · 2 seconds on expiry · 2 seconds on recent activity dates

---

## Scene 4 — Add new product

**Scene title:** Create a new product

**On screen:** Stock list → **New product** (or open `/products/new`) → fill Name, Category, Unit, optional Price, optional Barcode/GTIN → Create product

**Voiceover:**  
“To add a brand-new item, open the stock list and tap **New product**. Enter the product name, choose a category, and pick the unit — how you count it, for example syringe or vial. Price is optional. For barcode: leave it blank and the clinic will auto-assign an **INV** code like INV-0012. Or paste a manufacturer GTIN from the box — the app will still create an INV code and link that GTIN for future scans. Tap **Create product**.”

**Hold:** 1 second per field as Mohamed fills · 2 seconds on success / product detail

---

## Scene 5 — Link manufacturer unique barcode / GTIN (Data Matrix or QR)

**Scene title:** Link unique package code to an INV product

**On screen:** Product detail `/inventory/[id]` → section **Unique package codes** → Scan package to link **or** type GTIN → **Link**

**Voiceover:**  
“Every product has a clinic code starting with **INV**. Packages also have a unique manufacturer code. Point your camera at either the **square Data Matrix** on the side label, **or** a **QR sticker** if the box has one — for example a supplier sticker. Both can register the unique code to this INV product. On the product page, under Unique package codes, tap **Scan package to link**, or type the GTIN, then tap **Link**. Next time anyone scans that package, the app opens this product.”

**Hold:** 3 seconds showing camera aimed at Data Matrix · 2 seconds showing linked “Unique: …” under the INV code · optional 2 seconds on a QR sticker if available

**Example codes (for prep, not required on camera):**  
- Restylane Vital / Lidocaine → INV-0007 · GTIN `07331689121391`  
- Restylane Vital Light → INV-0008 · GTIN `07331689120523`

---

## Scene 6 — Add stock to Main Store

**Scene title:** Add stock (receive into Main Store)

**On screen:** Bottom nav **Add** → scan INV or GTIN / Data Matrix / QR → quantity → location **Main Store** → confirm Add

**Voiceover:**  
“To put new stock into Main Store, tap **Add** on the bottom bar. Scan the package — INV code, GTIN, Data Matrix, or QR all work once linked — or type the code. Enter the quantity, keep the location as **Main Store**, then confirm. You’ll see the quantity increase.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds after successful add · show confirmation briefly

---

## Scene 7 — Scan lookup

**Scene title:** Scan tab — look up a product

**On screen:** Bottom nav **Scan** → camera · allow permission if asked · scan or type → product detail

**Voiceover:**  
“Tap **Scan** anytime you want to find a product quickly. Allow camera permission if your phone asks. Point at the Data Matrix or QR, or type INV-0007 if the camera struggles. When the code is known, the product page opens. If nothing matches, the app shows the extracted code and offers **Create product** or open the stock list to link it to an existing item.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on Scan screen · 2 seconds after product opens

---

## Scene 8 — Transfer / Move to a doctor location

**Scene title:** Move stock from Main Store to a room

**On screen:** Bottom nav **Move** → scan/find product · From **Main Store** · To e.g. **Dr. Ahmad** · qty · Confirm transfer

**Voiceover:**  
“When a doctor needs stock, tap **Move**. Scan or find the product. Set **From** to Main Store and **To** to the doctor room — for example Doctor Ahmad. Enter quantity one or more, then tap **Confirm transfer**. Stock leaves Main Store and appears at that location.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on Transferred confirmation · optional glance at Home activity

---

## Scene 9 — Consumption / Use or Sale

**Scene title:** Record use or sale

**On screen:** Bottom nav **Use** → Consumption or Sale · location · qty · Record use

**Voiceover:**  
“When a product is used on a patient, tap **Use**. Choose **Consumption** or **Sale**, pick the location where it was used — often the doctor room — enter the quantity, then tap **Record use**. Stock at that location goes down.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds after record · show reduced qty if visible

---

## Scene 10 — Expiry date and Home alerts

**Scene title:** See and edit expiry · Home alerts

**On screen:** Product detail → Expiry · Edit product (admin/manager/head nurse) → Home → Expiry alerts

**Voiceover:**  
“Expiry matters for clinic safety. On any product page you’ll see the expiry date when it’s set. Managers and head nurses can tap **Edit**, update the expiry, and save. Back on **Home**, the **Expiry alerts** list highlights items that are expired or coming due soon — tap one to open it.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on product expiry · 2 seconds on Home expiry alerts

---

## Scene 11 — Product activity history

**Scene title:** Activity history with dates

**On screen:** Product detail → **Activity history** (add / transfer / use with date and time)

**Voiceover:**  
“Scroll down on the product page to **Activity history**. Every add, transfer, and use appears with the **date** and time, the location, and the quantity. This is how you check who moved stock and when.”

**Hold:** 3 seconds scrolling activity rows so dates are readable

---

## Scene 12 — Staff Reports (From–To, Print / CSV)

**Scene title:** Manager report with date range

**On screen:** Top bar **Report** (admin / manager / head nurse) → From–To dates · stock added · transfers · use/sale · **Print** / **CSV**

**Voiceover:**  
“Managers, admins, and head nurses can open **Report** from the top bar. Set a **From** and **To** date to focus on one period. You’ll see stock added, transfers, and use or sale by staff. You can **Print** or download **CSV** for the clinic records.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on date fields · slow scroll through sections · 1 second on Print/CSV buttons

---

## Scene 13 — Settings: rename location and category

**Scene title:** Settings (who can access)

**On screen:** Top bar **Settings** → rename location · rename category

**Voiceover:**  
“**Settings** is for admin, manager, and head nurse only. Here you can rename a location — for example if a doctor room name changes — and rename a category heading. Regular staff won’t see this button. Make a careful change, confirm, then return to Home.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on Settings · show rename fields without necessarily saving a live rename on camera

---

## Scene 14 — Quick tips

**Scene title:** Camera, HTTPS, typing INV codes

**On screen:** Scan or Add screen · tip text / manual INV entry

**Voiceover:**  
“Quick tips before you go. Allow **camera permission** when the phone asks. The app needs **HTTPS** — use the official clinic link. If the camera can’t read a code, type the clinic code like **INV-0007** and tap Go. Remember: Data Matrix on the side label **or** a QR sticker can both link to the same INV product. You’re ready to help keep Clinic Inventory accurate. Thank you, Mohamed and team.”

**Hold:** 2 seconds on manual INV entry field · end card with app URL
